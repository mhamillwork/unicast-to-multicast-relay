The relay is scheduled only onto nodes labeled node-role/multicast-gateway=true
and uses hostNetwork so multicast leaves on {{ .Values.config.egressInterface }}.

Senders can target:
  1. Service {{ include "mcast-relay.fullname" . }} (ClusterIP or externalIPs)
  2. The gateway node IP directly
  3. A keepalived / kube-vip / MetalLB VIP that lands on the gateway

Do not combine a load-balanced Service with an active/standby VIP for the same
stream. Active/standby senders must all use the VIP. Active/active duplicates
datagrams and receivers have to deduplicate.

Readiness is http://<node>:9100/readyz and metrics are /metrics.
